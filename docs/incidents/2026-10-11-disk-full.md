# Incident: disk full crashed the Mac growth fetch (2026-10-11 00:12)

## What happened
- **Before 00:12.** The Mac's data volume was already almost full: 188 GB of 228 GB used, with only a few GB free
  through the night. Several things kept writing to the same disk:
  - git worktrees, which totalled about 14 GB at the worst point, each holding its own build cache;
  - repeated Next.js production builds, about 0.6 GB of cache per build plus a `.old` copy while each rebuild runs;
  - the growth fetch's `projects.jsonl`, growing about 12 MB a minute;
  - embedding checkpoints, saved through a temporary copy.
- **What tipped it over.** The planner added an ~800 MB data snapshot and a ~400 MB Python virtualenv in its
  scratchpad.
- **00:12.** The fetch crashed with `OSError: [Errno 28] No space left on device` (`corpus/data/grow-fetch.log`).

## Impact
- The fetch was down until it was restarted, at about 00:18.
- Every line of `projects.jsonl`, `summaries.jsonl`, `progress.jsonl` and `rejected.jsonl` parsed as JSON, so no
  data was lost.

## Failure points
1. **Large temporary files went onto a nearly full shared disk** without checking free space first.
2. **Nothing warned before the disk filled.**
3. **Finished worktrees weren't removed.** About 8.6 GB was freed at 02:40 by removing six of them.

## Rules from now on
- **Check `df -h /System/Volumes/Data` before writing more than ~100 MB.** Keep at least 5 GB free while collectors
  run.
- **Remove a worktree as soon as its PR merges** (AGENTS.md, loop step 7).
- **Delete scratch snapshots and virtualenvs** as soon as they've served their purpose.
