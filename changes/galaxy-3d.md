## What shipped
The galaxy is 3D again: a perspective camera that moves like a map (drag carries what's under the pointer, the wheel zooms where you point, option/ctrl-drag turns, cloud labels fly to their cloud, idle drift after 20 s), and two layouts of the same stars behind a "By category / By meaning" toggle mirrored in `?layout=clouds|nebula` (default clouds). Switching flies every star across in about 1.4 s (instant with reduced motion). Glowing stars, cloud labels projected from 3D, idea arcs, fly-to on select, with the camera held on a selected project. The 2D viewport and its tests are gone. Zooming in sharpens the stars into separate discs (the glow fades out) and names the 10 nearest projects; prize rings stay a fixed thin width (#399).
