## What shipped
The scan: when an idea lands, the galaxy drains to grey, the camera frames it, and arcs reach its stored neighbours closest first; each landing flashes, lights its category cloud and tags the star with its real cosine similarity from the new read-only `GET /api/scan/<versionId>`. A readout logs each link and ends on `<n> projects searched · 5 nearest linked · closest <s> · 0 invented`, with Replay. An intro (`/?intro`, or "Run the intro" on the map) lights each category in turn with its real count. Any input ends either; reduced motion shows the settled state.
