# Research: UI for a brainstorming tool (2026-10-10)

Asked by the person: what UI works for this kind of app, so it never overwhelms and stays about brainstorming.
Summarised from a sourced web review; claims link to their source.

- **Progressive disclosure, at most two levels.** Show the few most important options first, the rest one labelled
  step away. A strict wizard fits poorly for creative work; a step list with the current step marked and every
  step revisitable fits better. NN/g: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/,
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/wizards/
- **Defer judgment; go for quantity; build on ideas.** IDEO's brainstorming rules. Evaluation apprehension lowers
  idea output even for people working alone (Diehl & Stroebe 1987). https://www.designkit.org/methods/brainstorm-rules.html,
  https://homepages.se.edu/cvonbergen/files/2013/01/Productivity-Loss-In-Brainstorming_Toward-the-Solution-of-a-Riddle.pdf
- **Positive critique framing.** "How might we…", "I like / I wish / What if". https://www.sessionlab.com/methods/i-like-i-wish-i-wonder
- **AI help can make ideas alike.** AI suggestions raised individual creativity but made outputs more similar
  (Doshi & Hauser 2024): neighbours should push toward open space, not copying. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11244532
- **Empty states.** One prompt plus a few tappable starters, gone for returning users. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/
- **Dense maps.** Overview first, then zoom and filter, then details on demand (Shneiderman); labels by zoom level
  (Nomic Atlas); focus on a neighbourhood and dim the rest (Kumu Focus). https://docs.nomic.ai/atlas/data-maps/topic-modeling,
  https://docs.kumu.io/guides/focus
- **Glass only for floating controls**, never behind content or stacked (Apple's Liquid Glass guidance, secondary
  source). https://www.createwithswift.com/liquid-glass-redefining-design-through-hierarchy-harmony-and-consistency/
