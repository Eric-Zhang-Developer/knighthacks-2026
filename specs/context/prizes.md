Source: the official prizes page and Hacker's Guide, transcribed into the team's notes on 2026-10-10.
Rules and judging: [event.md](event.md).

**"$X in cash" is not cash.** Every amount is the organizers' valuation of the prize items. No track pays money.
Best Overall (1st) "$203,680" is about $2.4k of Mac minis plus trophies; the other ~$200k is the listed value of a
Full Sail University full-ride scholarship *opportunity*. Credits are counted as cash too.
The headline is "$305,784+"; the tracks below add to $257,196, and the rest isn't listed anywhere we have.

Prizes are **per team member** (max 4). "x 4" below means one each for a team of four.
