## Judging rubric

Every project is scored 1 to 5 on five criteria. The official rubric link wasn't captured; this is the team's
paraphrase of it, not the official text.

| Criterion | What it rewards |
|---|---|
| **Originality** | A problem or approach the judge hasn't seen before. A 5 means "never seen this." |
| **Technical Understanding** | Not complexity: whether the team can explain how it works, admit which tools helped, and show what they learned. A 5 includes visuals like diagrams in the demo. |
| **Functionality** | Works end to end, reliably. A 5 means about 90% of what we claim works, smoothly. |
| **Design** | Looks good and is pleasant to use. |
| **Wow Factor** | Given sparingly: "would I show this to my friends?" |

The rubric scores *all* projects; high scorers are then sorted into the General Challenges.

## Judging format

- 2 min setup, **6 min judging and questions**, 2 min teardown. Hard stop when teardown begins.
- At least one team member must attend.
- Teams must sign up for and attend the judging room of **each** challenge they opted into: the General room for
  Overall and general tracks, plus one room per sponsor challenge. Miss a room, lose that challenge.

## Submission requirements (Devpost)

- One member creates the team and invites the others. Members missing from Devpost get no prizes.
- A **demo video** attached.
- Sponsor and category prizes selected (Overall and general tracks are automatic).
- A **public GitHub repo** link. Private or missing repo = disqualified.
- Recommended: screenshots, graphs or visuals, and a clear, detailed description.
- Register on Devpost with the same email used for event registration (needed for post-event feedback).

## Rules that change what we build

- One project per team, original work made for Knight Hacks.
- Prizes are per eligible team member (max 4), so a team of 2 gets 2 of each item.
- **Best AI Hack** must *feature* a model (ML, CV, LLMs, ...); being coded with AI doesn't count.
- **Most "Useful" Hack** is actually for the funniest, goofiest project.
- **Best First-Time Hack** needs more than half the team to be first-time hackers.
- Sponsor challenges were released at Opening Ceremony; each is opt-in and judged in its own room.
